About Me

PLATFORM PAYMENTS PROPTECH ECOMMERCE

I'm a product leader who loves turning complex problems into solutions that feel simple.

My career has taken me across fintech, proptech, e-commerce, payments, and consumer experiences. Along the way, I've had the opportunity to lead products processing billions of dollars, build experiences used by millions of people, launch new payment methods and platforms, develop new revenue streams, and lead talented product teams through everything from early discovery to large-scale launches.

What I enjoy most, though, is the process behind those outcomes: understanding a problem deeply, asking the right questions, bringing people with different perspectives together, making the hard tradeoffs, and watching something that started as an idea become genuinely useful to customers.

I'm a big believer that great product leadership isn't about having all the answers. It's about creating an environment where teams can find the right answers together — with clear strategy, strong customer empathy, healthy debate, good data, and a willingness to change course when the evidence says we're wrong.

I'm also endlessly curious about how things work. That's part of why this site exists. I enjoy experimenting with new technology, building prototypes, digging into data, learning enough SQL to be dangerous, and finding increasingly better ways to turn an idea into something people can actually experience.

Outside of work

I'm a husband and dad of two, which is easily my favorite job.

When I'm not building products, there's a good chance I'm exploring a national park with my family, making another trip to Disneyland, doing karate or gaming with my kids, rolling around with our dogs, or pursuing whatever new rabbit hole has captured my curiosity that week.
